GOAL:
Navigator helps a user clarify what they are trying to achieve and determines their current direction.

CREATE A /navigator ROUTE.

NAVIGATOR EXPERIENCE:

Step 1 — Goal
Question:
"What are you trying to achieve?"

Options:
- Start a digital product
- Build an audience
- Monetize a skill
- Turn an idea into income
- I'm not sure yet

This first question should NOT be skippable.

Step 2 — Starting point
Question:
"Where are you right now?"

Options:
- I only have an idea
- I have a skill
- I have an audience
- I already have something to sell
- I'm already making some income

Allow "Skip for now" on this step.

Step 3 — Strength
Question:
"What do you have most of?"

Options:
- Knowledge
- Creativity
- Audience
- Technical skills
- Time
- I'm still figuring it out

Allow "Skip for now".

Step 4 — Preference
Question:
"What kind of path sounds most useful?"

Options:
- Create a digital product
- Build content
- Offer a service
- Build a simple business
- Explore options first

Allow "Skip for now".

NAVIGATOR UI:

- Show progress at the top.
- Example: "01 / 04"

IMPORTANT PRODUCT BEHAVIOR:

Store answers in frontend state.

After completing Navigator, navigate to:

/navigator/result

Do NOT implement the final recommendation algorithm yet.
